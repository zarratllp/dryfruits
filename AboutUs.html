Who We Are

"We are a trusted dry fruit store established in 2023 by Mohammad Yusuf,dedicated to providing premium quality dry fruits at affordable prices. With a strong commitment to freshness, purity, and customer satisfaction, our journey began with a single vision — to deliver the finest selection of dry fruits to every household. Today, we proudly operate 4 branches across Bhiwandi, serving our customers with consistency, quality, and care. Whether it’s daily nutrition or festive gifting, we ensure every product meets the highest standards." “We believe healthy eating should be simple and delicious.

That’s why we offer naturally rich, nutrient-packed dry fruits with no added preservatives—perfect for your everyday wellness.” We source premium dry fruits directly from trusted farmers and regions known for excellence. Every nut, every raisin, and every bite reflects purity, care, and uncompromised quality.

Our commitment lies in hygienic handling, natural freshness, and delivering nutrition that families trust every day.
